About

Michael Ralph is a photographer, videographer and content creator based in Guildford, Surrey, working across live music, portraiture, landscape, architecture and motion.

His creative toolkit includes Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop and InDesign, together with DaVinci Resolve Studio for video editing and short-form content.
