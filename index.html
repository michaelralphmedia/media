03 · More ways to use the shoot

More than
one deliverable.

A show can create more than one piece of content.

Depending on the project, coverage can combine photography and video so the same event produces assets for immediate social use as well as longer term promotion.

01

BTS content

Arrival, preparation, soundcheck, backstage moments and the parts of the event the audience normally does not see.

02

Artist content

Photo and short form video created around the artist for show promotion, releases, social feeds and wider visual identity.

03

Photo + video

Combined coverage gives you a strong edited image set and a highlight video from the same event, so one shoot produces more content for promotion, social media and future marketing.
